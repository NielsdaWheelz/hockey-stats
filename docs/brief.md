# project brief

**how good was this player at creating and suppressing chances, where did it happen, and what evidence supports the estimate?** the product serves one hockey analyst on localhost. v1 estimates underlying level across a selected season, with observed results and game evidence alongside.

v2 adds **ability at a particular time, estimated in hindsight**. a reader choosing january 15 wants our best estimate of the player's level on january 15. games played in february or march can help answer that question. show the ability date and the date through which games informed the fit. a later publication can revise the estimate for the same january date. keep the season-level estimate available under its own label. [model interpretation](research/model-direction.md#retrospective-player-ability)

historical player analysis takes priority over forecasting. “how many wins would this player add next season if we signed him?” would warrant a separately named forecast with a decision date, expected availability and minutes by strength, a replacement reference and a goals-to-wins conversion. it would need testing on games played after the forecast date. peer rankings can already help locate disagreements worth investigating in the seasonal estimates. [comparison protocol](research/model-direction.md#comparing-skater-rankings)

## experimental product decision — 2026-10-22

build the complete selected player cards with real sources, computed components and working views. use what those outputs reveal to choose improvements. v1 covers the hockeyviz-style analytical skater, descriptive skater and goalie cards, and the jfresh-style comparative and microstat skater cards. the [field crosswalk](product-inventory.md#v1-field-crosswalk-and-card-acceptance) defines their scope. the [whole-card delivery record](research/application-v1-20/verification.md) records which obligations have real values, evidenced source gaps or an explicit deferral.

a result can be observed, reconstructed, inferred, or estimated by a model. keep those meanings visible through the page and export. wrong units, probability arithmetic, actor joins, timing, unmatched counts and exposure, or double-counted components invalidate the affected result. valid experimental estimates may have poor calibration, weak attribution, sensitive priors or unknown uncertainty. to claim supported ability, establish predictive adequacy, shared-deployment sensitivity and resolution for the actual quantity and population. ranks and peer distributions compare estimates. uncertainty about those estimates requires other evidence. [scientific rationale](research/model-direction.md)

## what the reader sees

in the three modeled seasons, enter through the league value table or player search. the comparative skater card leads, with recorded results one click away. the first 2026–27 refresh opens on recorded results and dated game logs. the current-season modeled headline follows a separate refit. show games through the observation cutoff and estimates through their own fit cutoff. [current-season views](product-inventory.md#v2-current-season-views)

use profiles or the comparison page to select two players of the same kind. skater peer groups default to reported position, with a 300-minute general threshold and a separate 50-minute specialty threshold on the designated support. thresholds govern viewing. retain a low-minute player’s available magnitude and explain a withheld rank. distinguish player effects, reference workload and actual deployment. [cohort rules](specs/v1-14-history-cohorts-exports.md#cohort-calculation)

in v2, selecting a teammate from deployment will show how their club played with both on the ice and with each apart. use only games both dressed for that club. the reader sees the ice time behind each team rate, including how little apart time some pairs get. [reciprocal teammate view](product-inventory.md#v2-reciprocal-teammate-view)

v2 skater discovery will let the reader find two players with similar net chance impact and different offensive and defensive estimates, or compare finishing with inferred setting. select points to inspect the underlying evidence and compare the players. [many-player scatters](product-inventory.md#v2-many-player-scatters)

when looking back at a skater, read his seasonal value or recorded shooting rate against the same year's position peers. show the actual years covered, his age, clubs and qualifying minutes. a player can hold his rank while his rate falls. the dated hindsight estimate answers how good he was at a particular point within a season. [annual peer context](product-inventory.md#v2-annual-peer-context) · [dated ability](research/model-direction.md#retrospective-player-ability)

keep the five compositions distinct. the analytical skater sheet integrates four full-rink maps, distributions and value accounting. the descriptive card explains scoring, usage and on/off results. the goalie card pairs stopping with saving evidence. the comparative png carries a compact interpretive footer and its two histories, with detailed provenance on the page and in csv. the v2 microstat page will show inferred setting beside passing and link transition to modeled on-ice zone movement. each tracked field keeps its own observation and coverage requirements. nhl edge gathering, verification and skating ranks were explicitly deferred. [microstat page contract](product-inventory.md#v2-microstat-page) · [source limitations](issues/microstat-inputs.md)

show recorded season totals through the published cutoff and reconciled game rows. the reader can inspect individual games. arbitrary browser-selected date ranges for observed aggregates fall outside the agreed interaction. an operator-selected snapshot fixes the included games for everyone who opens that publication. observed on-ice, personal and spatial rates have their own matched populations. show included minutes and coverage. for observed spatial maps, the included league-average difference leads, absolute rates are available, and fixed descriptive smoothing leaves raw bins accessible. share each selection and quantity across table, card, comparison, history and export. ## evidence, delivery and scope

build on public source evidence. preserve original bytes and retrieval identities. version subsequent corrections, imputations and transformations, with input selection and cutoff. missing measurements stay visible. no full-game video access is assumed. paid tracking and public redistribution require separate rights and scope decisions.

the destination includes each identified, analytically meaningful public input, documented peer-method family and agreed candidate. retain correct implementations even when inactive. track raw preservation, prepared facts, selectable encoding, exercised implementation, recipe activation and scientific support separately. evidence limitations have a source and reason. a deferral leaves the requirement open. [feature repertoire](model-features.md)

the destination is the selected public hockeyviz/jfresh player-analysis repertoire, with independent definitions where published methods stop. the [visual inventory](product-inventory.md#visual-coverage-inventory--2026-10-22) records which surrounding views are still open. stronger claims need predeclared, meaningful player comparisons and prediction budgets. research-exposed seasons and frozen study verdicts retain their meanings. [reference audit](research/player-card-references.md)

fit locally, publish explicitly and browse the installed sqlite publication with the external drive detached. keep one previous valid publication for rollback. bulk evidence and expensive fits live on the external drive. interruption can require a manual rerun. the operator chooses when to update. current-season operation needs a snapshot identifying completed games and observation/model cutoffs. local runs have no next-morning freshness promise. [system responsibilities](architecture.md) · [storage](storage.md)

the intended eventual public site serves a published copy independently of the macbook and its drive. launch timing and redistribution rights require a separate decision. prospects, contract/cap and fantasy analysis, team forecasts, lineup simulators and arbitrary date-window fits fall outside the selected private product. the [plan](plan.md) owns assignment and sequencing. earlier slice-specific choices, tradeoffs and acceptance boundaries remain in their specifications and the [decision history at v1 release](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/hockey-stats/blob/9d1b9fa8f77f79730dde112df1316180028fb293/docs/brief.md#choices-confirmed-by-the-user).
