problem: 04a's conditional empirical-bayes intervals and meaningful-band labels do not by themselves establish calibrated player claims. the corrected fixed-truth exercise shows shrinkage bias, undercoverage and prior-driven near-zero assertions for an unidentified contrast. the numerical repair does not resolve these scientific limits.

evidence: the corrected [simulation](/Volumes/Expansion/hockey-stats/runs/attribution-04a-20261014-02/simulation/simulation.json) binds 600 independent replicates and 6,000 annual fits. the [saved-evidence review](/Volumes/Expansion/hockey-stats/runs/attribution-04a-20261014-02/reviews/recovery-population-review.json) independently reconciles all 6,930 summaries, including exact truths and unavailable cases.

- in the mixed spatial case, the prespecified zero-sum offense difference `3−4` has cell0 truth `+2` attempts/60. its interval covers truth in 86/200 first-year replicates, wilson 95% interval `[.363,.499]`, and 179/200 fifth-year replicates, `[.845,.930]`. fifth-year bias is `−.459` attempts/60. its matched scalar truth is exactly zero; scalar recovery cannot establish spatial recovery.
- fifth-year entrant 32's matched spatial offense scalar covers truth in 167/200 replicates, wilson interval `[.777,.880]`, with bias `−.663` attempts/60.
- in the paired case, offense difference `26−31` has identical design columns and true matched spatial scalar difference `−4` attempts/60. its first-year interval falsely lies entirely within the scalar meaningful band in 36/200 replicates, wilson interval `[.133,.239]`. by year five, coverage is 197/200, `[.957,.995]`, but the estimated difference remains zero to numerical precision and every replicate is unresolved. wider intervals do not identify the two players separately.

each denominator is 200 independent replicates for one fixed case, contrast, response and year; none pools players or years. wilson intervals quantify uncertainty in these replicate frequencies. the working posterior intervals condition on fitted scales and were not promised uniform frequentist 95% coverage. these findings limit the empirical claims that can follow from them; corrected real-player resolution has separate saved-state and assessment verification; that software evidence does not remove these scientific limits.

impact: a precise conditional association or `within_meaningful_band` label cannot establish repeatable ability, empirical equivalence or successful individual identification. wide paired intervals can cover truth while the mean never recovers the true difference. innovation estimates can absorb effects of the working observation model; they cannot be interpreted as pure ability drift.

resolution: execute and independently assess the prospective 04b criteria, preserving this corrected benchmark. no retrospective multiplier, outcome-selected threshold or relabeling of conditional intervals closes this issue. closure requires evidence meeting the criteria for the claims actually proposed, or an explicit narrowing of those claims; game refits alone are not a bias or identification repair. assess this alongside [complete-rate adequacy](attribution-negative-rates.md). supported chance valuation and source/deployment robustness remain separate requirements.
