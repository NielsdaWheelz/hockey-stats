# comparative export density

problem: v1-15's required expanding evidence footer produces a very tall image for a product intended as a compact comparative card. this is a content-contract concern, not a finding that implementation failed its assigned specification.

evidence: [the verified export receipt](../research/application-v1-15/verification.md#native-rendering-and-saved-outputs) records complete and partial pngs of 1200 × 8623 and 1200 × 7009 pixels. it attributes the height to field/annual evidence. this follow-up is based on that receipt; no new visual inspection has been performed.

impact: the saved artifact is cumbersome to share and read at card scale despite retaining correct values and meanings.

proposed owner/change: the existing comparative content/export owner should specify a compact information hierarchy, retaining identity, season, headline/reference/workload, ten tiles, both histories, units/cohort keys and material missingness/prior limitations. keep detailed field/annual provenance and numerical evidence accessible in the page/csv rather than repeating all of it inside the image. reuse the prepared card and png owner; no second export framework, new producer or schema is needed. specify the exact content/size boundary before changing code.

tradeoff: the png would carry essential interpretation, not the full standalone audit detail. this proposed contract amendment requires its own assignment; historical v1-15 acceptance remains valid.

resolved when: native saved complete/partial/long-label examples demonstrate a legible compact card at the agreed size, retain both histories and essential meanings, and link through publication/method identity to exact detailed evidence without truncating or concealing material limitations. verify fidelity and existing export snapshot behavior at the affected boundary. this need not wait for numerical fitting and does not block producer integration.
