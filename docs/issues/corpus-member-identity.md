# derived game files can change under the same corpus identity

the [corpus writer](../../analysis/src/hockey_stats/corpus.py) writes each game envelope and records its relative path in the parent manifest. the manifest hash therefore fixes the inventory and paths while leaving the expected bytes of those envelopes unspecified. [corpus readers](../../analysis/src/hockey_stats/corpus_inputs.py) enforce membership and status. the [observed reducer](../../analysis/src/hockey_stats/observed.py) checks the expected root hash and records what it actually reads. [publication composition](../../analysis/src/hockey_stats/publication.py) joins modeled and observed inputs on season and root hash.

a coherent regeneration at one of those paths could make observed and modeled facts come from different revisions without changing the root selection. v1 had independent source and preservation checks. the audit found a contract gap, with no demonstrated mismatch in the delivered database. [v1 evidence](../research/evidence-v1-01/verification.md) · [release checks](../research/application-v1-20/verification.md#drive-backed-candidate-and-preservation)

the source and reader owners close this when a new parent selection binds the exact bytes of every emitted envelope, every consumer verifies that binding on the bytes it uses, and an absent envelope retains its own status. exercise unchanged facts, a valid but substituted member, and composition across revisions on small fixtures. preserve historical selections and their original identities. document which selections can support the stronger claim.
