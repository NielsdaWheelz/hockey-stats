# player-consequence budgets

status: open; owner: 04a attribution feasibility, with 03p owning its prospective representation decision.

problem: existing calibration tolerances do not bound error in player effects, and 03o's representation rule values predictive superiority without a practical allowance for useful components.

evidence: [03h](../research/chance-03h/decision.md) explicitly calls its margins project tolerances rather than player-value bounds. [03o](../research/chance-03o/decision.md) has all primary intervals crossing zero and no supported representation; this establishes neither equivalence nor acceptable predictive cost.

impact: research can optimize a probability metric without knowing which differences matter to the product, or mistake an inconclusive comparison for rejection of an interpretable representation.

resolution: define an independently justified player-effect resolution and bounded source-stratum perturbations; assess their propagation through a declared attribution model. use the evidence to specify prospective claim-specific margins and uncertainty before new assessment. retain historical failures and research exposure; no margin chosen to rescue them. [04a](../specs/04a-attribution-feasibility.md) and [03p](../specs/03p-chance-structure.md) remain stubs, not execution authorization.
