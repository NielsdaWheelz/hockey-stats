# player-consequence budgets

status: open; owner: 04a attribution feasibility, with 03p owning its prospective structural comparison.

problem: existing calibration tolerances do not bound error in player effects, and 03o's representation rule values predictive superiority without a practical allowance for useful components.

evidence: [03h](../research/chance-03h/decision.md) explicitly calls its margins project tolerances rather than player-value bounds. [03o](../research/chance-03o/decision.md) has all primary intervals crossing zero and no supported representation; this establishes neither equivalence nor acceptable predictive cost.

impact: research can optimize a probability metric without knowing which differences matter to the product, or mistake an inconclusive comparison for rejection of an interpretable representation.

partial decision, 2026-10-14: the user selected a meaningful 04a recorded-rate contrast of 5% of the relevant league-average team rate, separately for offense and defense. this is not an allowed-error margin or an expected-goals budget. the [04a specification](../specs/04a-attribution-feasibility.md) owns the choice; source-stratum perturbations, propagation evidence, uncertainty and acceptable distortion remain unresolved. this issue stays open.

the user separately selected [03p's 99% predictive-gain retention policy](../specs/03p-chance-structure.md#selected-comparison-policy) for mirror pooling within the existing decomposition. that research policy permits a bounded predictive cost for spatial simplification; it does not settle direct-versus-decomposed adoption or translate into player-value tolerances.

04a now includes a provisional post-fit comparison of final-season unblocked spatial effects under the existing direct/decomposed transfer models and a common reference. historical attribution stays in count-rate units; no historical chance refits are implied. this gives location-only sensitivity evidence. disagreement between these unadmitted models cannot bound error against truth or, by itself, close this issue.

resolution: operationalize the selected 5% recorded-rate contrast and define bounded source-stratum perturbations; assess their propagation through a declared attribution model. use the evidence to specify prospective claim-specific margins and uncertainty before new assessment. retain historical failures and research exposure; no margin chosen to rescue them. [04a](../specs/04a-attribution-feasibility.md) remains a stub; [03p](../specs/03p-chance-structure.md) specifies a separate structural comparison without resolving this issue or authorizing execution.
