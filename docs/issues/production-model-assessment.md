# predictive evidence for the released models

the released execution and positive 5v5 recipes have checked five-season fits, scores and publication. their incremental predictive value over simple controls on unseen games is open. the older research used different outcomes, likelihoods, references or support. its held-out intervals cannot establish current player accuracy. each numerical owner can test its recipe in a bounded comparison. freeze the game-level time split, observable targets, exclusion rules, trainable transformations, references, loss summaries and uncertainty interpretation before assessment. refit both arms on permitted training games only. compare execution with simple location/type/strength predictions conditional on the same information. report block-origin reconstruction separately. compare positive 5v5 lineup rates with a context-only or simple-history control on identical directed intervals, including all retained zero-event time. report paired game loss, calibration, coverage by strength and representative player consequences. give reconstructions and model-valued targets their own scores. agreement with a target generated by execution measures agreement with that valuation, and requires separate observable outcomes to support a claim about goals.

state the direction and limits even if a simple model wins. the five v1 seasons have prior research exposure. a genuinely prospective claim needs a cutoff fixed before later games. individual resolution and interval calibration require their own evidence. ## what knowing the goalie adds

use the execution benchmark's game cutoff. on identical later on-net shots, predict whether a goal was scored conditional on the shot reaching the net. retain actual attacking and coaching context for the two shot models. exclude the subsequent whistle, follow-up and outcome from predictors. fit models and choose priors, references and tuning settings using earlier games only. compare three predictions on each shot.

- the execution model with individual goalie effects
- a shared-goalie refit that omits individual goalie effects and re-estimates the same context terms, including measured workload and rest. zeroing an effect after the full fit gives a reference prediction and cannot supply this control
- a simple goal probability from each goalie's past save percentage, shrunk toward the training league rate for that strength

record eligible shots, goals and exclusions. compare paired log loss by game and observed versus predicted goals separately at even strength and on the penalty kill. report assessment uncertainty by game and say which fitted quantities it holds fixed. these targets use actual outcomes rather than the model's follow-up xg. [on-net probability](../specs/v1-07-goalie-continuation.md#actual-goalie-evidence)

before scoring, pick one well-used same-team tandem using training workload and overlapping shot contexts. fix the strength, common shot mix and meaningful margin in goals prevented per 100 on-net shots. evaluate both goalies on that mix. goalie a’s predicted advantage is `100 × (mean goal probability with b − mean goal probability with a)`. compare this with the difference in their held-out goals saved per 100 against the shared model, weighting each goalie's shots to the same mix. positive differences favor a in both calculations. report the actual sample sizes and overlap. this contrast concerns the tandem’s supported contexts. the card uses a league-wide neutral panel.

if overlap is inadequate, report unresolved. otherwise use a game-level interval for the held-out contrast, conditional on fits and weights. an interval wholly beyond the margin in either direction supports a meaningful ordering on these games. one wholly within the band supports a small difference. an interval crossing a boundary leaves the ordering unresolved. uneven start assignments and unmeasured shot difficulty still limit attribution.
