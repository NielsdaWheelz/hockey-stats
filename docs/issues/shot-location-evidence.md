# shot-location evidence

status: open; source semantics and imputation validation. identified 2026-09-29. inclusion through blocked-origin imputation is now settled by the user.

problem: a coordinate attached to an event is not necessarily the shot's origin. the intended all-attempt analysis includes blocked shots, but has not yet established the supported source semantics, origin estimator or its accuracy and downstream sensitivity.

evidence: hockeyviz's [xg 8 description](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/xg8) distinguishes block locations from shot origins and describes imputation for blocked shots and some tip locations. its [magnus 9 description](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/magnus9EV) uses inferred blocked-shot origins. these published observations motivate an audit; they do not independently verify the semantics or error rates of our future captures.

pr2 inspection, 2026-09-29: the two [admitted captures](../../fixtures/README.md) contain 77 blocks whose owners agree with roster-resolved shooting teams. 66 opposing-player blocks have reported zone `D`; 11 explicitly teammate-blocked attempts have zone `O`. a generic zone-to-owner interpretation or blanket ownership reversal would be wrong for these inputs. preserve recorded coordinates, zone and defending-side evidence in pr2; establish the analytical frame in reconstruction and shooting origins in chance valuation. these checks do not validate spatial accuracy or origin inference.

pr03 inspection, 2026-09-29: across three admitted games, all 111 timed blocks lack shot type and reported goalie id, while all 251 other timed attempts have both. these include non-5v5 attempts. [reproduction and implications](../research/statistical-methods.md#pr03-candidate-and-information-boundaries). a missing-shot-type predictor would reveal the outcome. the [03a specification](../specs/03a-chance-workflow.md) omits that predictor and specifies a joint origin/outcome candidate; its fixed forward kernel and origin prior remain assumptions needing scientific assessment in 03b. goalie identity comes from reported event membership, not this outcome-dependent field.

impact: treating a block coordinate as a shooting coordinate can misplace chance quality and player effects. excluding blocked attempts instead changes the modeled opportunity population; it is not a semantically neutral cleanup. neither silent relocation nor silently declaring the source coordinate correct is acceptable. finite fits and conserved probability mass do not identify unobserved origins; different kernel/prior choices can agree on observed outcomes and disagree spatially.

resolution: inspect attributed captures for each supported event/source version; document coordinate meanings and hand-check selected examples against independent evidence where available. specify the origin estimator and handling of missing, ambiguous, blocked, and tipped locations. retain observed fields, attributed inference rules, and evidence about reconstruction error or its limits. verify normalized origin weights, conserved event contributions, source/cutoff provenance, and downstream sensitivity of chance values, maps and player estimates. distinguish fixed-imputer results from uncertainty propagated through imputation. evaluate the same population contract throughout the pipeline. an unblocked-only benchmark does not resolve the selected blocked-origin requirement.

blockers: the [external audit](../research/external-corpus-audit.md) led to the user's choice of fresh three-season captures; the entire fresh three-season corpus is now admitted and source-checked. 03b retains insufficient independent physical-origin evidence; declared opportunity/origin stress stability fails in both development and frozen confirmation. [the scientific decision](../research/chance-03b/decision.md) rejects the declared use and withholds 04; later player-map sensitivity requires supported upstream values and its own validation. keep this issue open until its applicable evidence requirements are met; published methodology or fixture checks alone do not resolve it.

03b source/scientific inspection, 2026-09-30: [the frozen protocol](../research/chance-03b/protocol.md) selected four block examples before fitted maps across long/wing/central/teammate-blocked geometry, but all came from one game. queried replay endpoints returned existing event metadata or unavailable responses, yielding no independent release annotations or spatial uncertainty. this limited search does not prove no public replay exists. a source-only own-half block and unblocked tip were also retained before maps; native API/report agreement establishes only those recorded proxy semantics. post-discrepancy development backhand/snap examples and the first three development missing-type goals (own-goal descriptions) remain explicitly diagnostic selection, not independent release evidence or a population claim about all missing types. confirmation repeats the frozen source checks: its first inspected missing-type goal has no own-goal descriptor; the next two do. no type is filled or excluded.

[the decision](../research/chance-03b/decision.md) identifies raw/source examples, finite-rink kernel displacement and point-value sensitivity. absolute mass, event totals and normalized posteriors conserve numerically, but those invariants do not establish physical accuracy. [origin/value sensitivity](origin-value-sensitivity.md) tracks the separate instability findings. closure still requires representative independent release geometry/uncertainty and a supported scientific interpretation; player-map robustness remains a later 04 responsibility, contingent on chance support. no unblocked-only substitution was made.
