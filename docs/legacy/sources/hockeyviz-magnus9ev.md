## 1. Summary

Magnus 9 (EV) is McCurdy's regularized-regression "isolation" machine for NHL 5v5 play: it estimates each skater's individual, context-stripped impact on shot generation (offence) and shot suppression (defence), plus coach, score, zone-start, venue/period, rest, and post-penalty effects, all simultaneously. Its signature design choice is that the regression's observations and coefficients are not scalars but **shot-rate maps** — functions over the offensive half-rink discretized onto a hex grid, with each shot weighted by xG (from his xG model, "xg8") and expressed relative to league average. Play is segmented into "stints" (5v5 passages with no substitutions); each stint yields two function-valued observations (home attacking, away attacking), producing ≈500k rows × ≈2k covariates per season. The model is fit by **generalized ridge regression** with three loss terms: fit-to-data, deviation-from-prior (last season's estimate per skater, aging-adjusted), and deviation-from-zero/league-average (structural), solved in closed form. Identifiability is achieved by large sum-to-zero group penalties rather than reference categories, so every term reads as "relative to league average"; second-difference smoothness penalties tame the per-second zone terms and per-minute score terms. Priors are elaborate: sequential season-to-season fitting since 2007-08, a parabolic age-24-centered aging adjustment applied to both prior means and prior certainty, an entry prior for never-seen players, a positional recentring to stop selection-bias drift, and a twins-only fusion penalty (the Sedins). Versus Magnus 8, version 9 applies aging more thoroughly, caps zone-start terms at 35 seconds, improves smoothness, and deletes the goaltending rebound terms. The article is explicitly the EV (5v5) sibling; power-play/short-handed play is out of scope here except for the "penalty shadow" terms covering 5v5 immediately after an expired penalty. Evaluation is informal: penalty values justified by "rough out-of-sample testing", and a residual diagnostic (raw on-ice minus model expectation) whose net year-to-year correlation is 0.02 over 18 seasons.

## 3. Model structure sketch

- **Layer 0 (upstream, separate model):** the xG model ("xg8") supplies per-shot danger weights and the blocked-shot origin imputation. Magnus 9 consumes xG-weighted shot locations; shooting/goaltending talent is *not* part of this model (map values read "given average shooting and goaltending talent").
- **Layer 1 (this model, single joint GLS/ridge fit per season):** one simultaneous function-valued regression containing —
  - per-skater offence map + defence map (the isolates; the headline outputs);
  - per-coach: general O/D pair, score-state O/D pairs (−2…+2), shell O/D pair (3rd period, tied/up 1–2);
  - non-human context: venue×period (6), score×minute×venue (840), zone-start×second (4×35, attackers only), rest (3×O/D), penalty shadows (2).
  - Everything conditions on everything else within the one linear fit; "isolation" = the marginal β for each column after joint fitting.
- **Prior chain (across seasons):** season t's skater estimates → aged (parabolic, vertex 24) → positionally recentred → become β₀ for season t+1, with age-dependent certainty Λ; entry prior for new players; chain bootstrapped in 2007-08 from an iterated age-average prior.
- **Post-hoc compositions (not in the fit):** QoT (×4), QoC (×5), total skater effects, synthetic coach impact (overall + 0.2·shell), player-level residual diagnostic (raw minus model expectation).
- **Not covered here:** PP/SH play (this is the EV article; the "(EV)" suffix and figure directory `m9figEV` imply sibling non-EV versions — (inferred)); goaltending (rebound terms removed in v9); injuries; shooting talent.

## 4. Full covariate list (as stated)

Paired offence/defence terms:
1. Skaters: 2 columns each (offensive impact, defensive impact).
2. Coach, general impact: attacking/defending pair per head coach.
3. Coach, score states: attacking/defending pairs for trailing-by-2, trailing-by-1, tied, leading-by-1, leading-by-2 (no terms for |diff| ≥ 3).
4. Coach, shell: attacking/defending pair, active only when tied or leading by 1–2 in the 3rd period.
5. Rest: well-rested / normal / tired, each with offence and defence maps (fractionally coded by share of skaters in each state).

Offence-side-only terms:
6. Zone (shift-start) impacts: {OZ, NZ, DZ, on-the-fly} × per-second terms for seconds 1–35 of a shift (live text's "240 columns"/"sixty terms" is stale v8 wording; v9 = 140 terms). Fractionally coded by share of attacking skaters and spread over the seconds covered by the stint (per the commented example).
7. Game time & venue core: Home 1st/2nd/3rd, Away 1st/2nd/3rd (6 columns).
8. Score impacts: 840 columns = {home, away} × minute 0–59 × {≤−3, −2, −1, 0, +1, +2, ≥+3}.
9. Penalty shadows: 2 columns (team previously short-handed; team previously with more skaters), for 5v5 after penalty expiry before any stoppage.

Total ≈ 2,000 covariates; every coefficient is a hex-fabric map.

## 5. Evaluation protocol and reported results

The live article contains **no formal held-out map-loss section**. What it does report:
- Penalty magnitudes (skater Λ ballpark; coach 100,000) justified by "rough out-of-sample testing" / "better out-of-sample accuracy than at least one larger and at least one smaller value" — a coarse, informal grid.
- Formal generalized-cross-validation-optimal penalties (per MacDonald §5.3) were computed and **rejected**: they are much smaller and yield "wildly varying (and hence unphysical) year-to-year estimates of player ability" — i.e., temporal stability is used as a validity criterion over pure predictive optimality.
- Residual diagnostic: player-level residuals (raw on-ice minus model expectation) are ~20% smaller in scale than Magnus 8; year-to-year correlation of net residuals = **0.02** over 18 seasons (read as: what the model leaves unexplained is non-persistent).
- **[source-comment-only]**: "Out-of-sample testing on the last five seasons gives a mean absolute error of 0.6320 xG per game (for both teams combined)." — present in the HTML but commented out of the live page; possibly stale (Magnus-8-era); do not cite as a live Magnus 9 result.
- Face-validity exhibits: home advantage per period (e.g., home 2nd period ≈ +9.3% xG/60), score-effect summaries, zone-start decay curves, fatigue asymmetries, and the Taylor Hall worked decomposition (the latter in a commented-out results appendix using 2016-18 data).

## 8. Open questions / ambiguities in the source

1. **Stale zone-term counts:** the live Covariates section says "two hundred forty columns" and "sixty different terms … through the thirty-fifth second" — internally contradictory; What's New, the Zone section (35 terms), and Group Penalties (140 total) confirm the v9 truth is 4×35. The 240/60 wording is unedited Magnus-8 text.
2. **Player structural-penalty value:** the numeric diagonal K value for skaters (10,000) exists only in commented-out text; the live page never states it (only that the coach value 100,000 is "considerably higher"). Unclear whether 10,000 is still the v9 value.
3. **Response construction detail:** the 10-ft Gaussian smoothing and league-average subtraction are described only in the commented-out worked example; the live Response section describes hex assignment and per-60 normalization but not the kernel. Whether v9 still uses the 10-ft Gaussian (vs the "technical improvements, giving better smoothness") is not stated. (Our own memory treats Gaussian-10ft as display-kernel-only — the article does not resolve this either way.)
4. **"xG per sixty" units for hex counts:** the live text jumps from "hex where it was recorded" to "units of xG per sixty minutes" without spelling out the per-shot xG weighting step (it's implied via the xg8 link and the "given average shooting and goaltending talent" gloss). (inferred: each shot contributes its xG value, not 1.)
5. **No formal evaluation:** no held-out map loss, calibration, or predictive comparison is published in the live article; the only quantitative out-of-sample number (MAE 0.6320 xG/game) is commented out and of uncertain vintage.
6. **Aging constant provenance:** c = 25 bp and vertex 24 are conceded to be without theoretical justification; the claim that his older curves are ~2× too shallow is an argument from circularity, not a new measurement.
7. **Entry-prior asymmetry vs old replacement level:** live entry prior is (−10%, +10%); the commented-out scheme was (−7.5%, +1%). Whether the change was deliberate recalibration or the numbers' provenance is not documented.
8. **PP/SH siblings:** the article never states where PP/SH Magnus 9 documentation lives; the "(EV)" title and `m9figEV` figure paths imply siblings exist (inferred), but scope deferral is implicit only.
9. **Penalty-shadow scope:** whether shadow terms are per-team-role only (2 columns) or interact with anything else is unstated; also no duration cap is given ("before any whistle or stoppage").
10. **"Sixty terms per score state" phrasing:** the Score results section says "sixty terms for each score state for each team" (matching 840 = 2×60×7), but the same section's gallery labels are per-minute maps; harmless, but the term "sixty" refers to minutes, not seconds — easily confused with the zone-term seconds.
11. **Λ off-diagonals beyond the Sedins:** none are used; the fusion mechanism is described generally but exercised exactly once.
