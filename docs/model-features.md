## implemented local inventory

the [original sixteen numerical families](research/features-03k/ledger.md) retain their source and encoding contracts. each recipe owns its population and any adaptation to different strengths. the execution design above selects eleven.

| use in released execution | families |
|---|---|
| active game and prior-event context | `game_additive`, `recent_additive`, `recent_interactions`, `recent_geometry`, `off_wing`, `sequence` |
| active deployment and exposure history | `shift_age`, `shift_start`, `workload`, `calendar_rest`, `strength_transition` |
| retained source-backed alternatives, inactive in execution | `game_detail`, `recent_detail`, `action_windows`, `zone_run`, `historical` |

`historical` encodes lagged factual counts/exposure. annual borrowing operates on fitted player states. `sequence` describes continuity of a defending unit. `action_windows` counts prior actions over 5/15/30 seconds. inferred setting, defending continuity, window counts and elapsed possession each have different meanings. shooting hand, venue, source/report clocks, official identities, reasons/modifiers, schedules and biographical observations retain their own evidence and timing boundaries. home team alone cannot identify a rink. a new or changed design declares available-at-event information, treatment of later reconstruction evidence, applicable structural zeros, source conflict and missingness. estimating scales/imputations or selecting parameters uses training evidence only. individual clock cutoffs matter even within a recorded second. elapsed-rate fitting keeps affected counts and seconds together. a refitted ablation needs comparable support and reference conditions. zeroing fitted coefficients measures a different sensitivity.
