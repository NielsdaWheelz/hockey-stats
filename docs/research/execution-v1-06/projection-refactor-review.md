scope: the exact 5v5 fixture panel from the saved 2024–25 v1-05 state, with 801 physical attempts, 154,071 origin rows and the same 40 skaters before and after. this is a cost and arithmetic check, not a real-season capacity measurement.

the prior projection evaluated full panel prefixes three times per skater: focal masses, setter masses and focal stage ratios. a temporary counter observed 122 `_joint_log_prefixes` calls for 40 skaters plus neutral work. the refactor evaluates one log-prefix pass per focal skater in fixed 65,536-origin chunks; it derives setter setup mass from the neutral upstream prefix and the changed setup goal logit. the common neutral reduction uses the same chunk order in producer and detached reader. the integration remains log-domain through stage ratios, including representable conditionals when unconditional mass underflows. no origins, players, strengths, source weights or fitted coefficients were changed.

on the same fixture panel and people, uninstrumented projection time fell from 4.30 s to 2.65 s. `tracemalloc` observed 26,666,435 peak bytes allocated during the refactored projection after the panel was built; its saved arrays occupy 8,647,208 bytes separately. the refactor retains three float64 neutral vectors, 24 bytes per origin, plus small per-event reductions, and bounds working arrays by the chunk size. chunks end at physical event boundaries. at the reviewer's illustrative 22.5 million-origin season panel, the retained origin vectors would occupy about 540 mb plus bounded working arrays and output maps. this extrapolation does not measure a real season or the writer's panel-building and serialization peaks.
