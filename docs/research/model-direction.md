date: 2026-10-08. status: user-approved direction with retained-feature amendment; documentation only. [brief](../brief.md) owns settled choices, [plan](../plan.md) sequencing, [03j](../specs/03j-chance-experiment.md) the next experiment and [feature inventory](../model-features.md) the repertoire. implementation and fitting require their separately completed specifications/assignment.

## purpose and evidence

recorded hockey → release/outcome model → reference-standardized opportunity → season-level player effects → cards/maps. prediction, measurement, valuation and attribution are different questions. current research mainly addresses prediction; latent-release accuracy and player robustness remain separate obligations.

[03i](chance-03i/decision.md) finds broad conversion/all-attempt overprediction and little relief from fixed month/type composition. this weakens that composition explanation; it establishes neither a unique avoidance cause nor the failure of all interpretable modeling. lower proper losses remain improvements. no existing verdict changes.

## council judgment and approved order

three independent reviews challenged method alignment, statistics and product/systems. the method reviewer favors the restricted avoidance test; the statistician favors a cheap conversion-scale benchmark because avoidance cannot change conversion or no-recent predictions. the systems/product review wants a correct restricted objective and a decision that advances player analysis. these are disciplinary judgments, not quotations from mccurdy.

approved order: **03j conversion-scale benchmark first; 03k retained feature preparation separately; then one justified native experiment.** 03j's full handoff specification uses saved outputs and need not wait for repertoire implementation. the user confirmed adjustment-fitting uncertainty: resample both windows and refit the two parameters, holding the saved base model fixed. preserve the avoidance experiment as a candidate, not an automatic next fit. each experiment states mechanism/competing explanation, compute/numerical limit, success/failure interpretation and next obligation. no repeated general residual inventory or automatic recipe matrix.

the benchmark tests temporal portability of two probability-scale parameters. it cannot identify whether improvement comes from drift, shrinkage or missing mechanisms, and it does not authorize attaching a correction to standardized opportunity/player totals. a useful result nominates native research.

for avoidance, prospectively distinguish blocking level from allocation with the seven-category observable score. consider exact restricted marginal optimization with updated blocked weights, rather than renewed full joint em. objective agreement and measured convergence govern the choice; local reachability checks are preflight, not another diagnostic slice.

## published alignment and limits

[xg8](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/xg8) uses four shot-outcome layers, historical information, type-aware coarse/symmetric geometry and structured context. these differ from our joint forward-origin model. [magnus8](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/magnus8EV) and [magnus9](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/magnus9EV) remove terms when predictive evidence is adverse. retain feature capability while selecting fit terms empirically. the september 2026 [preview](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/preview2627) announces rewrites; older descriptions do not establish current private-model parity.

measurement has its own owner: inspect venue/source/type patterns before correcting them. [scorer-bias methodology](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/scorerBias) separates recording from home/away hockey effects. our [type drift](../issues/recorded-shot-type-drift.md), [contact-law geometry](../issues/block-kernel-goal-geometry.md) and [origin-value sensitivity](../issues/origin-value-sensitivity.md) remain open; none is proved to cause the calibration failures.

## retained challengers, selected only when justified

| idea | discriminating question / limit |
|---|---|
| on-net × goal given on-net | do additional observed outcomes separate conversion errors? existing source evidence needs explicit preparation; added layers do not guarantee accuracy |
| coarse/mirrored geometry or selective actor/type pooling | does less freedom improve support? trades detail and possible genuine asymmetry for stability |
| temporal/context representations with historical borrowing | do season-specific effects or broader joint context improve transport? preserve useful history; association is not proof of pooling error |
| observable-first origin/outcome parameterization | can directly modeled unblocked mass/contact observations outperform the present factorization? a structural challenger, not a release-accuracy proof |
| alternative origin/contact laws and tip proxies | do conclusions survive different measurement assumptions, including an inverse geometric control? varying one forward kernel's knobs does not bracket shared defects |
| matching and deliberately wrong synthetic contact laws | can apparently good prediction coexist with wrong recovered origins? tests estimation/identification under assumptions, never real-origin truth |
| measurement portability across teams/venues | does a pattern follow hockey participants or recording context? deployment and venue remain confounded; evidence before correction |
| continuity-based pressure | does verified defending-unit persistence explain sequences better? event rosters alone do not establish continuity or possession |
| player assumption envelopes | which magnitudes/signs/maps survive admissible origin laws? 04 owns this; envelopes are not confidence intervals |

fitted-model-conditional intervals omit fitting, selection and origin-law uncertainty. all three inspected seasons remain research-exposed; freezing a new protocol does not turn them into untouched confirmation. [adaptive evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629)

calibration tolerances are declared project choices; historical failures stand. future acceptance must support the intended probability and player claims, including consequential magnitudes/signs/maps, without retroactively relaxing a rejected model's rules. [calibration distinctions](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12916-019-1466-7)
