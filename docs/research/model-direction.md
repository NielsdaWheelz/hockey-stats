## published alignment and limits

[xg8](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/xg8) uses four shot-outcome layers, historical information, type-aware coarse/symmetric geometry and structured context. it describes single-season fits with dynamic penalties informed by preceding seasons; our joint multi-year fit with shared geometry/context is different. [magnus8](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/magnus8EV) and [magnus9](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/magnus9EV) remove terms when predictive evidence is adverse. retain feature capability while selecting fit terms empirically. the september 2026 [preview](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/preview2627) describes nineteen suitably weighted regular seasons and announces rewrites; this does not establish a nineteen-year stationary pool, a prerequisite for v1 or current private-model parity. a sequential annual-prior or recency-weight redesign remains separate from 03n.

measurement has its own owner: inspect venue/source/type patterns before correcting them. [scorer-bias methodology](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/scorerBias) separates recording from home/away hockey effects. our [type drift](../issues/recorded-shot-type-drift.md), [contact-law geometry](../issues/block-kernel-goal-geometry.md) and [origin-value sensitivity](../issues/origin-value-sensitivity.md) remain open; none is proved to cause the calibration failures.
