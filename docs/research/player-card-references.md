## jfresh and the current hockey stats product

the skater card reviewed on 2026-11-23 uses patrick bacon's topdownhockey model. jfresh weights the three most recent seasons for predictiveness, sums the projected war components, then ranks forwards and defenders separately among players with over 200 minutes of 5v5 in the latest season. finishing enters the forward headline and is omitted from the defender headline. goals and primary assists are projected 5v5 rates per 60. the 2022 explainer described an 82-game pace. the current guide leaves the numerical weights and precise workload assumptions unspecified. [current card guide](https://hockeystats.com/about/player-cards) · [2022 explainer](https://jfresh.substack.com/p/2022-nhl-player-cards-explainer)

topdownhockey also publishes single-season offensive and defensive even-strength war. the published method scales estimates by minutes played, uses situation-specific replacement levels, and includes 3v3, 4v4 and 5v5 with both goalies. the public top-100 summary shows component wins to two decimal places and labels one column “toi.” its selection, displayed precision and unspecified strength denominator limit comparisons with our 5v5 rates. [season leaderboard](https://hockeystats.com/war) · [war methodology](https://hockeystats.com/methodology/war)

the public skater preview shows skills, context, percentile histories and a download control. our card ranks the ledger's summed goals and keeps magnitude and units beside standing. its headline defaults to 300 included 5v5 minutes. the goalie preview and its open definitions have their own record.
