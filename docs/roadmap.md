# v2 roadmap

v1 assembled five private player cards through 2025–26. the next release should let a reader open a 2026–27 player, including a recent debut, and see what happened in the games played so far. a separate refit will update the modeled headline. the first new model lets the reader ask whether a skater’s impact on 5v5 attempt rates changed between january and march. [post-v1 review](research/v1-review.md) · [delivery sequence](plan.md#next-full-spec-queue) · [other prs](plan.md#remaining-v2-pr-boundaries)

## product usefulness

- **open this season on recorded results.** show the game log, matched minutes, shots, goals and supported rates with the played-through date and coverage. a rookie's available numbers can precede eligibility for a percentile. comparisons and exports use the same selection. keep the three prior modeled seasons accessible. [current-season views](product-inventory.md#v2-current-season-views)
- **read a card or compare two players without fetching the league's evidence.** the publication reader supplies season/person identity, eligible exposure, required scalars, availability and cohort inputs. full named evidence stays available for the selected player. preserve raw values, ties, sort order, histories, comparison and downloaded content. measure first and repeat navigation, detail opening and export on the same publication, including bytes, memory and time to usable controls. the first change can use the shipped seasons. [measured cost](issues/profile-history-payload-cost.md)
- **make the differences legible.** the card's content designer owns hierarchy, visible precision, comparison reference and material limitations across page and png. the numerical owner supplies exact values and definitions. on the microstat page, put the existing 5v5 setting readout beside passing and link transition to the same skater’s four modeled zone-movement roles. readers can use those estimates while seeing exactly which actions have tracking coverage. keep full-rink maps visible and detailed evidence one action away. review real complete, partial, low-support and mixed-position examples at ordinary card width. [microstat page contract](product-inventory.md#v2-microstat-page) · [readability](issues/card-readability.md)
- **show what a goalie stopped and what the model attributes to him.** first give each saving row in the csv its actual shot population. then put stopping on 100 common on-net shots beside goals saved above expected on the shots faced. the reader should see how including misses can reverse a season result and which events qualify as a recorded stoppage or follow-up. [export defect](issues/goalie-export-population.md) · [goalie card contract](product-inventory.md#v2-goalie-card)
- **see how a team played with two teammates together and apart.** open a pair from the deployment list. compare recorded 5v5 attempts for and against in games both dressed for the same club. offer reference xg on its own scored minutes. the three published seasons already support this work alongside the observed refresh. [reader contract](product-inventory.md#v2-reciprocal-teammate-view) · [edmonton example](research/v1-review.md#one-pair-two-game-selections)
- **find skaters who reach similar totals in different ways.** use the compact annual read to plot 5v5 chance creation and suppression, then finishing and inferred setting. inspect an extreme on the player's sheet or send two points to the existing comparison. team and position filters keep each player's season-wide estimate and its model reference. [discovery contract](product-inventory.md#v2-many-player-scatters) · [montreal example](research/v1-review.md#what-two-axes-reveal)
- **see what changed for a player and for his peers.** put each season's common-workload value or recorded shooting rate beside the median and middle half for qualified skaters at his position. age, clubs and included minutes put the three season marks in context. this extension to profile history can use the installed release as soon as the compact annual read is ready. [reader contract](product-inventory.md#v2-annual-peer-context) · [matthews and carlo](research/v1-review.md#when-value-and-standing-diverge)
- later views answer different questions. reciprocal rink maps need matched location coverage. a combination spider asks how rates vary across overlapping groups of several skaters. smoothing a game log needs a defined observation, time window and treatment of its edges. [visual inventory](product-inventory.md#visual-coverage-inventory--2026-10-22)

## data and operation

- **publish two dated selections.** preserve a schedule and original source versions at the first assigned capture. bind each derived game to its bytes. after another game finishes, prove it enters the later results exactly once and that the earlier totals reproduce even after a correction. distinguish unplayed games, completed games with missing detail, excluded time and true zero. [snapshot acceptance](issues/current-season-snapshot.md#two-cutoff-acceptance)
- **measure a workable cadence.** try operator-triggered weekly observations and, once the refit exists, monthly estimates or an on-demand fit. the last full build took 4.9 hours and admission another 59 minutes. use the two real refreshes to set the observation cadence and the first real refit's cost to set the modeling cadence. [release costs](issues/publication-iteration-cost.md) · [operating procedure](operations.md#switch-the-listener)
- **account for the data we lose.** trace dominant reasons for excluded ice time and changes in recorded shot types to original game records. count the affected minutes or attempts before changing a source rule. this can proceed alongside the first refresh. [source findings](research/v1-review.md#findings-that-change-what-we-build)
- **shorten the repeat cycle.** time preparation, conversion, publication and validation separately. start with the most expensive repeated reads/checks or a presentation correction that needlessly regenerates numerical output. protect the same semantic and physical invariants with equivalent set-based or ordered checks. record real elapsed time, resource costs and equality or intended differences. the one-user operating model remains local native processing, external expensive outputs, explicit activation and a single rollback publication. [release measurements](research/application-v1-20/verification.md)
- **make the next cutover predictable.** settle the file-identity check at its owner, with a small real-path rehearsal and fail-before-serve checks for wrong bytes, validator and schema. keep expensive full admission explicit and ordinary startup bounded. retain a few fast checks for touched stable contracts. [metadata incident](issues/macos-publication-file-identity.md) · [testing policy](specs/07-lightweight-testing.md)

## model credibility

- **estimate ability at a particular time with hindsight.** begin with the skater's 5v5 impact on attempt rates for and against. use games before and after the selected date, within the fit's declared evidence cutoff. deliver a selectable dated history on the card and in its export. check how well it recovers stable and changing skills in a simulation, including a gap in playing time. later candidates include finishing and goalie stopping, each with its own exposure and time scale. [temporal target](research/model-direction.md#retrospective-player-ability)
- **fit the season played so far.** start each refit from the same preceding annual state and use eligible current games once. publish the covered span, fit cutoff, reference and prior dependence. applying fixed historical coefficients to new shots would answer a separate question about chance quality. [season-to-date target](research/model-direction.md#season-to-date-estimates)
- **compare today's recipes with simple alternatives on the same evidence.** fix the execution benchmark at the first assigned current-season capture so later games supply a prospective test. compare execution heads with a location/type/strength model on matched outcomes. report reconstructed blocked origins separately. compare positive 5v5 lineup rates with a context-only or simple-history control on matched elapsed time, including intervals with zero events. report paired game losses, calibration, coverage by state and the player decisions that change. [prospective game cutoff](issues/production-model-assessment.md#prospective-game-cutoff)
- **test whether knowing the goalie improves the estimate.** compare goals allowed on the same later on-net shots using individual goalie effects, a refitted shared-goalie model and past save percentage shrunk toward the league rate. assess prediction separately at even strength and on the penalty kill. check whether the evidence distinguishes one preselected tandem at a meaningful gap in goals per 100. the study can find predictive value across the league while leaving the tandem's order unresolved. [assessment and decision rule](issues/production-model-assessment.md#what-knowing-the-goalie-adds)
- **investigate rank reversals one component at a time.** compare released skater creation and suppression with topdownhockey's seasonal even-strength measures on matched players. inspect raw magnitudes and role minutes before interpreting a reversal. use explained and unresolved cases to direct model review. the held-out comparison answers the predictive question independently. [ranking protocol](research/model-direction.md#comparing-skater-rankings)
- **ask which individual differences the data resolve.** a small known-truth case with an entrant and inseparable linemates complements the predictive check. on saved values, trace extreme low-minute skaters, territorial value from movement to successor goals and the large cancelling actual-context allocations. compare current evidence with carried history. use sensitivity language appropriate to the calculation. a good lineup prediction alone cannot distinguish two players who always share the ice. [scientific rationale](research/model-direction.md) · [documented allocations](issues/spatial-actual-context-extremes.md)
- a choice between fitted recipes needs a stated question, matched comparison and a criterion set before scoring. new source facts and inactive encoders can be implemented and exercised under their own contracts. stronger player claims need evidence for the specific quantity, population and uncertainty. a simpler model winning is a useful result. the assessment can proceed beside product and source work. calibrated player uncertainty will require additional evidence.

## decisions after the first results

choose the next increment from measured use, source coverage, build costs and player judgments that change. for deeper histories, price the two earlier prepared seasons by quantity. recorded results need reconciled facts and exposure. a modeled total also needs its annual projections, reference and component accounting. label each series with the span actually published. [earlier-season coverage](issues/experimental-visual-coverage.md#earlier-season-coverage)

the [feature repertoire](model-features.md) retains full-history/decay/attention, rink and tip corrections, richer time context and age/transition alternatives. the [product inventory](product-inventory.md) retains richer goalie views and the wider visual catalogue. skating acquisition remains user-deferred. forecasting, wider public distribution and team/roster tools each need a distinct product decision.

the first investment in missing tracking, if selected, is the primary pass leading to an attempt. a verified passer earns a shot assist on the tracked attempt and reference expected assists where the attempt has a reference xg value. measure how well inference identifies the passer and recovers player rates against a simple deployment baseline on held-out games before committing to a larger collection or publishing a new estimated rate. [pilot and decision rule](issues/microstat-inputs.md#first-tracking-study)

first deliver the observed refresh across two real cutoffs. the wider v2 milestone also calls for a quicker profile-to-comparison journey, a retrospective ability curve and one reproducible current-recipe comparison. the curve can use completed seasons, and the benchmark can be inconclusive or favor the simpler control. the season-to-date headline has its own follow-up and acceptance. [model refit](issues/current-season-fit.md)

the shipped v1 receipts remain the comparison baseline.
