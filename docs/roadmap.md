## philosophy

build correct computations and honest meanings before requiring mature estimates. units, sources, exposure, chronology, probability arithmetic and component accounting remain strict. weak calibration, prior sensitivity, uncertain attribution and unavailable calibrated intervals stay visible on experimental outputs. observed facts remain distinct. evidence supports particular claims; there is no universal admission switch that prevents using the private product.

implement compatible source-backed families in coherent batches. retain their extraction and select named recipes, then ablate by refitting when useful. avoid both endless coefficient-by-coefficient studies and an indiscriminate feature cross-product. missing observations stay missing or become explicitly inferred quantities; synthetic interface fixtures never count as a real analytical family.

completeness has two independent requirements: comprehensive input/feature coverage against the dated public-method/source inventory and named candidates, and actual component-to-screen coverage for every required panel, chart form, table, control and export. the [visual inventory](product-inventory.md#visual-coverage-inventory--2026-10-22) prevents a broad label such as maps from closing missing subviews. predictive value is not an implementation prerequisite. raw preservation, preparation, encoding, exercised behavior, recipe activation and scientific assessment are distinct; an inactive feature may be complete, but an unwritten required feature is not. unavailable evidence needs an explicit reason, and a deferral does not close the checklist.

complete the agreed repertoire incrementally while using it. inspect actual cards, tables and maps to choose consequential improvements. no study is assigned merely because the previous study finished, and no scientific threshold is retrospectively relaxed to rescue a failure. existing frozen verdicts remain evidence.

destination completeness and v1 completion are different. v1 does not wait for every alternative encoder or standalone visualization. its card fields need explicit definitions and source-to-screen behavior; an impossible observation may remain a labeled limitation, while an available but unimplemented component remains work. full private-model or private-tracking parity is not claimed. this buys earlier whole-card feedback at the cost of later surrounding views and incomplete tracking coverage.

exhaustiveness is a dated coverage claim. use the required-card crosswalk and its linked contracts to deliver v1 through many reviewable prs. later unrelated discoveries extend the destination inventory without automatically reopening the release; omissions from promised cards and correctness defects still need repair. [the field/card acceptance rule](product-inventory.md#v1-field-crosswalk-and-card-acceptance) owns that boundary; [the plan](plan.md#v1-delivery-sequence) owns the cuts and dependencies. contract-ready producer and interface work proceeds in parallel, with whole-card real-output acceptance before claiming completion.

the 2026-10-22 [v1 card crosswalk](product-inventory.md#v1-field-crosswalk-and-card-acceptance) is complete at the definition/source/owner level. twenty bounded [handoffs](plan.md#proposed-pr-boundaries-and-dependencies) now connect source facts, four numerical families and five card compositions. actual delivery receipts remain open; further edge work is explicitly user-deferred, leaving its two selected measurements unfinished. absent tracking remains a separate evidence limitation. neither delays the other card fields. no broader alternative-encoder or private-formula survey precedes implementation of settled v1 contracts.

milestones overlap. a usable experimental component may be inspected or explicitly activated before the repertoire is complete. stronger claims and public launch are distinct decisions: an honestly labeled experimental product can be shareable without every quantity being scientifically certified.

## scientific work within the product

[03p](research/chance-03p/decision.md) supplies a developmental chance representation; [04n](research/attribution-04n/decision.md) supplies evidence of useful complete regional prediction with unresolved native behavior and negative-rate defects. these are reusable starting points subject to compatible targets and explicit new use, not admitted ability or automatically publishable artifacts. [completed 04o](research/attribution-04o/decision.md) finds unresolved regional gain, higher native inverse-share loss and small common-reference player point changes under its fixed score/time adjustment. its frozen protocol and adverse evidence remain intact; its outcome is not a prerequisite for the experimental program. [model direction](research/model-direction.md) preserves the rationale and completed comparisons.

keep these distinctions explicit:

- fitted goal probability, reference-standardized opportunity and observed goals are different quantities;
- chance-valued attribution needs valued event responses and matched exposure, not an unexplained multiplication of old shot-rate effects by average xg;
- signed player effects are valid; negative complete expected rates are a model defect, not something to hide by clipping;
- a probability layer, explanatory subskill or grouped summary is not additional value to add again;
- estimates can be experimental before uncertainty is calibrated; missing uncertainty must remain missing;
- forward/defense peer references, league context and replacement valuation answer different questions.

use the [supported-attribution stub](specs/04-player-attribution.md) for stronger ability claims, not as a blocker for the experimental [publication](specs/05-publication.md) and [website](specs/06-website.md). required computational checks belong to each implementation. selected lasting regressions follow [07](specs/07-lightweight-testing.md); a new general harness is not a prerequisite.

## after the complete baseline

prioritize by consequence for the player's interpretation, rather than the next available modeling technique. the initial specification already requires positive complete-rate models, explicit actor standardization and contribution accounting. refinements include better source/inference quality, chance calibration, context and age/history assumptions, entrant/change behavior, more useful component estimates and uncertainty. choose the actual question from baseline evidence. do not preassign a new sequence of studies or require every candidate refinement.

current-season operation adds explicit acquisition, scoring/refit cutoffs and operator-run updates. historical expansion adds admitted seasons and comparable outputs, not archived website builds. both remain local-batch work. public hosting gets its own small specification when the product is worth sharing; no accounts, subscription system, remote training or freshness promises are implied.

## deliberate boundaries

our headline is underlying level across a season. hockeyviz's [instant-state description](https://hockeyviz.com/howto/isolate) and jfresh's [predictive card description](https://hockeystats.com/about/player-cards) are useful references, not interchangeable targets. reproduce capabilities under our explicit definitions; do not claim numerical or exhaustive private-product parity. [reference research](research/player-card-references.md) records evidence and uncertainty.

complete player analysis does not include every neighboring product: team/league dashboards, game/series reports, standalone coach evaluations, forecasts, roster simulators, prospects, other leagues, fantasy and contract valuation remain unselected. coach inputs to player models are included. the microstat card and supported fields are v1; paid tracking acquisition remains optional. arbitrary date-window fitting remains excluded. playoff/extra-strength recorded views retain separate scope decisions. private formulas are not required, but in-scope panels need independent contracts or a documented disposition; candidate status cannot silently close them.

keep the same operating model: ordinary local files and python fits, effect application behavior, sqlite publication, detached reads, explicit stop/copy/restart and one previous database. preserve expensive fits and source evidence; rebuild the website from git. breadth belongs in hockey capability, not operating machinery.
