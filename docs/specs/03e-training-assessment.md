## purpose and ownership

determine which claims the revised fitted model supports, rejects or leaves unresolved. software completion and source recovery cannot establish usefulness. preserve the original [03b protocol and rejection](../research/chance-03b/decision.md); new requirements must be justified prospectively for the new candidate's intended use.

reuse native fit/evaluate/score commands and the retained scientific comparison work in `analysis/research/chance_review.py`; shared numerical diagnostics belong in `chance_evaluation.py`. extend only for specified evidence. no second estimator, tuning service or generalized experiment platform.

inputs are versioned 03d software/configuration, explicit corpus selections, 03c coverage evidence and an approved protocol. outputs are retained expensive fits, attributable diagnostics/comparisons and a written decision under `docs/research/chance-03e/`, with supported scope and remaining limitations. an eligible final retrospective fit and 04 handoff are conditional outputs, not promised success.

## decisions before execution

- define the claims, populations, benchmarks and practical consequences before choosing gates. assess factual conversion/composed probabilities separately from standardized reference opportunity; an observed block's zero goal outcome cannot directly calibrate its positive reconstructed opportunity.
- specify a bounded development recipe, temporal isolation and selection rules. all 2023–24 through 2025–26 results have been exposed; recapture, relabeling or new folds cannot make them untouched confirmation. choose and disclose a justified assessment/confirmation design; absent suitable evidence, narrow the claim or retain insufficiency.
- define relevant calibration groups, proper-loss comparisons, sparse-data behavior and dependence treatment. freeze consequential criteria before evaluating their designated assessment evidence. later changes require an explicit revision, not silent rescue of a failed candidate.
- assess selective source coverage and recording comparability across seasons. compare origin and spatial-value sensitivity on compatible populations/references, separately for blocked and unblocked attempts. conditional intervals, assumption sensitivity and independent physical-location evidence answer different questions.
- define final-fit history/reference construction, information cutoff and event/exposure compatibility for 04. adding opportunity context must not silently remove the player mechanisms the product intends to measure.

03d design handoff: assess its two-stage candidate, seasonal actor shrinkage, shared type geometry and explicit source/context exclusions. the reference uses target-season training matchups; hold that population/weighting fixed across matched sensitivity recipes. factual calibration concerns recorded-context probabilities, not a demonstrated live forecast. report excluded goals, original rare types and coverage selection, not just pooled model groups. examine retained tip proxies, block-kernel/origin/refit effects and omission judgments named in 03d. measure exact reference-scoring cost before considering an approximation. no old 03b gate is silently reused or revised.

completion means an attributable decision with preserved evidence, including rejection or insufficiency when warranted. retain scientific assessment tools and results; delete only temporary software-test code under the existing workflow. the cost is another real-corpus study and potentially inconclusive evidence. neither a deadline nor fixture success warrants weakening a claim's requirements.

content design owns report structure, captions and explicit denominators, with clear supported/rejected/unresolved wording; scientific owners define populations, quantities and judgment.
