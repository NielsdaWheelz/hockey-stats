## hypotheses to retain

this is a ranked research backlog, not a promised feature bundle. diagnosis can change the order; origin sensitivity has its own claim-specific obligation.

| priority | candidate | reason / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | season-specific recent-context effects with historical borrowing | context residuals change between training and later games; retain useful history rather than repeat the failed history ablation |
| 2 | split conversion into on-net probability and goal given on-net | existing outcome labels distinguish missing the net from scoring; on-net includes goals; test predictive and explanatory value before changing the contract |
| 3 | one constrained simplification: shared left/right conversion effects or pooled tip-shooter effects | test whether symmetry or removal of weak actor detail improves generalization; each is a separate hypothesis, and pooling loses detail |
| 4 | original shot-type distinctions or richer preceding-event geometry | wrap-around and tip-in/deflected may disappear in current pooling; retained coordinates contain more than zone labels. block coordinates are contacts; integer event clocks do not measure puck speed |
| claim-dependent obligation | origin-law sensitivity; a simpler geometric blocked-origin imputer is one possible control | test whether spatial conclusions depend on the chosen origin law, even if observable fit is good; select the needed control, not a mandatory extra model. inverse origin-given-block weights are not a forward block-given-origin kernel |
| deferred | longer sequences, rest/workload/shift age, older seasons or new inputs | require a specific residual mechanism, trustworthy coverage and continuity/cutoff contracts; more data/features is not an automatic remedy |

these are hypotheses informed by our [diagnosis](../research/chance-03f/diagnosis.md) and published [hockeyviz shot methods](https://hockeyviz.com/txt/xg8), not claims of production-model equivalence. do not replace candidate-origin features with observed block-contact features.
