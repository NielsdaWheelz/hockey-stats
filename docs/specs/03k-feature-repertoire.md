## temporal derivation rules

shift facts examine EVERY report-compatible interval link: before-only selects before-interval rows; after-only after-interval rows. EACH player's age is `event_seconds-shift.start_seconds`; zero only when that player's row starts now. under `both`, coherent row/start/age must agree or remain unavailable. zero-duration, overlap, incomplete coverage or uncertain identity does not establish a shift. preceding-shift gap is same-period game-clock time, not intermission/wall rest. faceoff start needs unique compatible faceoff/shift-start membership; proven absence is `no_same_clock_faceoff`, not on-fly. faceoff zones in the focal shooting team's frame: defensive `x<-25`, neutral `-25<=x<=25`, offensive `x>25`. unknown/ambiguous evidence stays unavailable. keep supported membership when age is unavailable; exposure is unchanged.

per shooting team, start a defending-continuity sequence at a supported normal timed attempt, including non-5v5 source attempts. exclude shootouts/penalty shots/unresolved physical goals. index is `1+earlier counted attempts` in that sequence; age is time since its first attempt. follow initial defender shift rows through EVERY interval and endpoint; survivors only shrink. leaving/reentering cannot restore them. verified exhaustion makes next index 1; unknown span/boundary makes index unavailable. recover after verified complete turnover; period transition resets. stoppage/opponent action alone is not turnover.

action windows instead stop at existing `_RESETS`/period boundaries and include earlier records with gap ≤ window, even same-clock records when order is supported. `prior_zone_run` counts consecutive preceding non-reset records whose resolved locations all have `x>25`, stopping at a known different zone/reset; uncertainty interrupts with unavailable status. its start/age refer to those recorded actions, not continuous puck presence. a known empty run has count 0 and not-applicable start/age. this definition does not inherit persistent-defender boundaries.

window counts are retained for both teams separately, relative to focal shooting team (`same,opponent`); unowned/unresolved relevant actions make affected counts unavailable, not silently omitted. `_RESETS` is the existing explicit set: goal, stoppage, penalty, period-start/end, game-end, shootout-complete. recent-attempt means a same-team preceding normal attempt under current ≤5s rules; recent-saved-shot means preceding `shot-on-goal` with supported goalie presence; outside-zone means its normalized `x<=25`. these are recorded proxies, not physical continuation labels.

prefix toi integrates supported intervals before the focal clock, with all-strength/5v5 completeness separate. earlier same-clock event counts follow validated order; no future totals. return-to-5v5 needs contiguous known preceding membership and stops at a reset; it is a strength-transition proxy, not proven penalty expiration. penalty awards remain independently observed facts.

history scopes are previous appearance/game, calendar windows `[D-7,D)`/`[D-14,D)` intersected with the current regular season, season-to-date before D, and each of two previous complete regular seasons. `calendar_gap_days=D-prior_date` (integer); it is not days-off/elapsed hours. complete inventories define required evidence. sum attributed prior-game boxscores/exposure, not full current-season bio totals. missing earlier appearance is left-censored when corpus history is incomplete; when the complete named scope proves no appearance, return not applicable, not invented rest.

prior-one/two-date flags mean exactly `D-1,D-2`: positive-toi player appearance or team inventory game, respectively. no dates invented from scheduled hours. per-player `previous_game` is its previous observed positive-toi appearance within the declared scope; team previous-game is its earlier inventory game. any relevant missing evidence which could change that predecessor makes it unknown.

next-event is the immediate next validated chronological record; next-stoppage is the first subsequent stoppage in the same timed period before a period/game terminal. retain intervening identities/kinds. known terminal without a stop is not applicable; incomplete order/timing is unavailable. `next_same_team_attempt_within_5s` reverses the existing immediate-predecessor rule, never skips intervening actions to seek a shot. `goalie-stopped-after-sog` and delay are source labels, not validated freeze/rebound truth. `shot-on-goal` is a recorded non-goal result, not a physical-save assertion when goalie membership is unsupported.
