# pr04a — attribution feasibility and player-consequence sensitivity

status: **stub; not ready for implementation.** research direction approved independently of chance-model admission. [plan](../plan.md) owns assignment; [04](04-player-attribution.md) retains supported chance-valued player outputs and release requirements. full population/model/budget choices remain to discuss.

## target behavior

test whether genuine 5v5 stint data support understandable player-rate attribution, then identify which upstream errors materially affect player conclusions. outputs are research benchmarks and sensitivity evidence, not admitted chance-creation ability or a 04 handoff.

start from recorded all-attempt rates for scalar benchmarks and recorded unblocked-coordinate patterns for spatial benchmarks. independently define each numerator and compatible exposure; coordinate absence must not unnecessarily exclude scalar attempts. retain time with no events, both goalies, membership ambiguity and selective coverage. block contacts are not release coordinates.

use synthetic known-effect stints to test recovery, correlated deployment and uncertainty behavior under stated assumptions. real deployment patterns may inform simulation; simulated truth stays labeled. define a small named family of probability/location perturbations to assess changes in player magnitudes, signs, comparisons and maps. provisional or historically rejected chance weights, if included, are sensitivity inputs only.

## contract and ownership

inputs: attributable interpreted events, separate event/elapsed membership, aligned exposure, identities/history where selected, and declared simulation/perturbation assumptions. outputs: reconciled benchmark populations, fitted research associations, population-specific scalar/spatial accounting, recovery checks, sensitivity results and consequences for future admission criteria.

each spatial map must reconcile to its own population-matched scalar. the recorded-unblocked spatial benchmark need not equal the recorded all-attempt scalar benchmark: their numerators and populations differ.

reuse reconstruction, corpus, references and feature primitives. python owns attribution/numerics; no effect-side hockey calculation. a simple explicit estimator is the starting candidate; exact model, regularization, historical borrowing and uncertainty belong to the full spec. no generic attribution framework or new source campaign.

content must distinguish observed counts, recorded-rate associations, simulated effects, provisional chance weights and supported ability. sensitivity envelopes are not confidence intervals; simulation/repeatability does not establish physical-origin truth or causal separation.

## decisions for the full specification

- choose the first scalar/spatial benchmark, season/corpus, event-to-exposure rules, reference, units and treatment of entrants/trades/missingness. do not inherit chance-model exclusions mechanically.
- choose a useful player-effect resolution before inspecting candidate sensitivity results. evaluate meaningful magnitudes and spatial conclusions rather than require precise ordering of adjacent players.
- specify one bounded attribution design, controls and synthetic recovery cases. align zero-event time, teammate/opponent exposure and deployment covariates; do not control away mechanisms players should receive credit for.
- predeclare source-stratum probability/location perturbations, bounds and reporting. do not choose tolerances from the residuals or intervals needed to rescue a known failure. close the [consequence-budget issue](../issues/player-consequence-budgets.md) only with attributable evidence.
- state uncertainty conditioning and which claims the evidence cannot support. an assumption envelope supplies no physical-location accuracy claim.
- assign shared-module changes separately from 03p and select a few relevant lasting checks under [07](07-lightweight-testing.md). keep temporary campaign tests/controllers disposable.

completion: population/exposure and each population-matched map/scalar calculation reconcile; synthetic recovery and the declared sensitivities are assessed; remaining identification/source limitations and a concrete next decision are recorded. a failed or inconclusive feasibility result is valid. no rejected model is admitted, no final player card is supplied, and the all-attempt product remains the destination.

tradeoff: this branch begins the actual player question earlier at the cost of provisional outputs and parallel research. physical origins, supported chance valuation, player robustness and explicit publication remain separate obligations.
