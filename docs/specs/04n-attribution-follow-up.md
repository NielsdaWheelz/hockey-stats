- fit volume from the same locatable-unblocked counts, retained exposure and training games as shape. do not substitute all-attempt volume or full-season 04a/04b estimates.
- compose each arm as `complete rate = predicted volume × reference cell share + predicted shape departure`. reuse exact 04m shapes only when population, references, split and player/context contracts remain compatible.
- the candidate budget on unchanged inputs is six new scalar states: five annual borrowing states and one independent final state. source changes can require fresh matched shape states; specify that branch separately rather than silently enlarging the run.
- assess paired complete regional error differences, total-volume performance and the signed volume–shape interaction. regional component losses are not simply additive. retain native diagnostics and independently reconcile composition. no absolute regional mse/rmse is implied by the saved paired moments.
- inspect complete-rate signs, negative mass, minima and affected exposure through original retained row/design evidence. signed player effects may be negative; complete negative rates are not event intensities. no clipping, automatic positive-link rewrite or invented zero-negative-cell gate. broad regional adequacy is primary; native behavior remains visible.
