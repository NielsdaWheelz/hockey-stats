# pr05e — spatial view contract

status: **later stub; not ready for implementation.** [the plan](../plan.md) owns sequencing/assignment. pursue hockeyviz-style spatial explanation in the existing player experience. this stub records the missing contract; it does not assign a spatial estimator or make 05d a scientific prerequisite.

## intended capability

paired for/against rink views with explicit orientation, quantity, units, reference, shared comparison scales, coverage and access to definitions. native spatial resolution, visual smoothing and statistical resolution are different choices. distinguish absolute observed rates, differences from a declared baseline and adjusted player effects.

the current observed publication has no spatial records. choose one bounded deliverable before writing the full spec:

- **source-supported observed maps:** publish counts/locations with matched exposure and explicit missing-location exclusions. an unblocked-only target must be named; it does not replace the intended all-attempt ability target. block contacts are not shooting origins, and imputed locations are not observations.
- **analytical view design with synthetic fixtures:** explore the eventual supported card/map presentation with prominently labeled synthetic examples. no withheld real fitted values or unlabeled fixture panels enter real profiles. concrete fixture design is separately assigned; this stub does not authorize another component framework or showcase application.

## boundaries to specify

python owns population, coordinates, aggregation, any spatial baseline and inference. effect owns coherent publication transport; the frontend renders the declared values. reuse reconstruction/coordinate and publication owners. specify geometry, location meaning, per-cell versus density units, exposure, missingness, zero/unavailable cells and metadata before choosing the visual treatment.

content design owns color/sign conventions, absolute versus difference legends, comparable scales, accessibility and clear separation of observation, imputation and estimated ability. independently check orientation, conservation, game/season reconciliation and rendered values; smoothing cannot manufacture precision.

real adjusted maps still require supported analytical outputs under [04](04-player-attribution.md) and [05](05-publication.md), then extend the same application under [06](06-website.md). design can precede that evidence; release cannot. no new capture, model admission, numerical campaign or publication is assigned here.

tradeoff: observed maps provide real spatial evidence but require a new publication/population contract; synthetic analytical designs can proceed earlier but may need rework. neither establishes isolated player contribution or resolves blocked-origin uncertainty.
