## identity and reported usage

one `person` record supplies every card: selected season, id/name, season teams, reported position/position group, sweater numbers by team, supported handedness, birth date and age at february 1 of the season's ending year. optional normalized height is centimeters, with display conversion only; [unverified admitted units](../issues/bio-height-units.md) leave it null while preserving raw height. conflicting/missing identity fields remain null with source reasons. portraits/team artwork are optional presentation assets; absent assets neither block the card nor trigger remote fetching. salary, contract and projected-role labels remain excluded.

`reported_usage` contains positive-toi appearance count, summed known boxscore seconds, minutes per appearance, reported shifts and listed/supported/missing game counts. minutes per appearance is `sum(seconds)/(60*positive_toi_appearances)` on the same known records. zero-toi roster listings are not appearances; a missing record is not zero. traded players aggregate their actual team appearances once. retain team breakdown and incomplete-coverage flag.

supported/missing game counts describe toi coverage. shift coverage is independent: `reported_shifts` is null unless complete over the listed games, with missing-game count in support reasons; do not present a partial sum as complete. preserve observed skater display name/position and every old affiliation when enriching identity. `person.teams` may add dated source-supported affiliations unavailable to old observed reduction; existing observed season/team facts stay exact. require prepared and dressed-game evidence for additions, never a present-day roster. unassignable game/person evidence remains coverage, not invented appearances. reported goalie inputs remain reusable, but v1-07 owns the composite goalie publication and its usage header.

`strength_usage` contains reconstructed seconds and appearance counts for `ev` (`5v5/4v4/3v3`), `pp` (`5v4/5v3/4v3`), `pk` (reciprocals), and `other` supported states, with exact states and a separate `5v5` child retained. both goalies are required for ev/pp/pk; extra attackers belong to other. role-conditional minutes/appearance in detail count games with positive known time in that group. the card stacks absolute included minutes, never those different-denominator averages. show included minutes per appearance and the denominator, not a falsely exhaustive partition of official toi. unknown elapsed support and reported-versus-reconstructed differences remain visible. the descriptive card may say ev for this union; the isolated skater model still says 5v5.

usage uses supported membership independent of shot/credit completeness. a missing actor or coordinate cannot remove valid toi. scoring and each microstat instead own their matched count/time populations.

## scoring and recorded goal context

`scoring_by_strength` records goals, primary assists and primary points (`goals+primary_assists`), with counts, matching seconds, per60 and coverage for the same groups. extend the existing reconciled credit owner; do not infer assist order from season totals. credited assistants who have left the ice retain their legitimate credit. zero elapsed time gives a null rate, not invented time. unclassifiable strength retains a separate unclassified count; it cannot enter a supported strength rate. all-strength official totals remain separately labeled source evidence, not a fallback for these reductions. descriptive bars show goals plus primary assists at ev and pp; 5v5/pk/other/counts are accessible detail. current 5v5 personal values remain their existing authoritative fields. secondary assists/total points remain preserved source evidence; new derived secondary-assist/full-point rates are not required by these reference bars and cannot tighten existing goal/a1 support.

the `five_on_five` group and exact 5v5 state both copy old personal counts/time/support. ev sums that component with separately matched 4v4/3v3 components; other groups sum their disjoint states. calculate group rates from summed counts/time, not mean rates. broader prepared ordinary-attempt masks cannot replace the old 5v5 component. preserve unknown/unclassified support and legitimate off-ice credit separately; no fabricated time or observed row.

add `recorded_goals` to the context population union, starting from existing recorded-all-attempt support. require supported team event strength/on-ice assignment and reliable goal/on-net accounting, not personal shooter/assist eligibility; remove affected interval counts and time together on a further conflict without changing old all-attempt facts. retain otherwise valid zero-event time. reduce goals for/against and on-net attempts for/against on identical support. `shooting_for=goals_for/on_net_for`, `shooting_against=goals_against/on_net_against`, `save_fraction=1-shooting_against`; zero denominators give null. these are recorded ratios, not fitted finishing or goalie effects. scatter annotations use `100*(for/(for+against)-.5)` percentage points from an even share, unavailable if both counts are zero.

the descriptive card's two scatter panels use (a) existing matched all-attempt for/against per60 and (b) this recorded-goal for/against per60. each shows focal on, focal off in dressed team-games, and its matching included-league reference. use that population's own time; y-axis may run downward but stored against values keep their sign. no score adjustment, causal claim or substitution of xg for goals. on/off xg detail, when selected, uses the separately named neutral-valued population.

league references aggregate each physical team-side interval once. player duplication must not multiply league counts/time. a traded player's team reference pools their dressed team-games; opposite sides retain their team identities. display included minutes and gaps for on/off/reference independently. team and league guides in the descriptive lower panels use these same population rules, not model-neutral references.

## recorded penalties and deployment

recorded `penalty_drawn`, `penalty_taken`, `penalty_served` count source-attributed infraction credits, one per unique source event, separately from modeled team-award targets. a double minor counts once; distinct same-clock events remain distinct. require participant/team evidence; retain original type/description/duration. count categories are mutually exclusive; bench/unattributed/conflicting and any explicitly supported coincidence evidence remain separate dispositions. neither coincidence nor advantage awards are inferred from clock equality.

role dispositions are `attributed|not_recorded|unresolved`. absent optional drawn/served keys, or a source-explicit bench penalty without an individual taker, are `not_recorded`: zero recorded credit, not proof nobody drew/served it. supplied null/malformed/conflicting ids, absent individual taker or contradictory structured identity/team evidence are unresolved. complete means structurally usable api recorded-event evidence with supplied roles checked against dated identities, not independent proof of every infraction. report-role comparison is a manual acceptance sample: the existing prepared reports have descriptions, not parsed role ids. no new free-text report parser or claimed automatic role agreement; demonstrated contradictions remain attributed gaps.

drawing/taking bars use all-strength infraction credits per 1,000 reported minutes on player-games with usable role evidence and valid positive reported toi. retain off-ice attribution; served is detail, never taking. missing ledger excludes affected game counts/minutes. unresolved taking/serving excludes the offending team's player-games for that role; unresolved drawing excludes the opposing team's; unknown offending team implicates both. other resolved roles survive. keep unknown-person and zero-toi credits outside the rate with reasons. team/league references pool matching player-game credits/player-minutes, not physical time. modeled ordinary-minor contrasts remain different fields.

`deployment` retains all valid 5v5 shift starts, including unknown start type. coalesce contiguous records for the same player before classifying starts; an intervening stoppage does not create a new shift without a real participation break. classify a corroborated faceoff start as offensive/neutral/defensive zone; a verified live-play start is on-the-fly; otherwise unknown. mere absence of a same-clock faceoff proves neither on-the-fly nor zone. zone bars use start count divided by all supported shift starts, with on-the-fly/unknown shares visible in detail; never re-normalize only offensive+defensive starts to 100%. team/league guides use the same denominator convention.

the actual shift start must have supported 5v5 state; a later transition into 5v5 during the same participation run is not a new start. deployment team/league guides pool player starts/seconds over corresponding dressed-team/league player-games, not physical team-time.

score deployment is seconds leading/tied/trailing at the source-supported pre-event score, divided by known score-state included 5v5 seconds. split elapsed rows when the score changes; keep unknown seconds separate. with `K=leading+tied+trailing` and `U=unknown`, known-state shares divide by `K`; `unknown.share=U/(K+U)` is a separately labeled coverage fraction. zero denominator gives null. show trailing/leading bars and tied/unknown detail. do not count events instead of time or give a goal's new score to preceding play. v1-03 repairs both typed validators to enforce these different denominators.

counterpart usage is a recorded deployment proxy, not teammate/opponent ability. for each counterpart `j`, take `u_j` from `reported_usage.minutes_per_appearance`. for relation teammate/opponent, compute `sum(shared_5v5_seconds_ij*u_j)/sum(shared_5v5_seconds_ij)` over known `u_j`, excluding the focal player from teammates. retain known/all slot-seconds, unique counterparts, F/D breakdown and missing reasons. complete teammate/opponent slot-seconds equal respectively 4/5 times matched focal time. partial known means are labeled; unknown usage is never zero.

the descriptive context scatter uses the same usage values ranked league-wide within F/D, descending, player-id tie break. with `T` teams in the season inventory, forward bands are ranks 1..3T, 3T+1..6T, 6T+1..9T and the rest; defender bands 1..2T, 2T+1..4T and the rest. shared-slot-second-weighted band indices give typical forward/defender coordinates for teammates and opponents; missing role support gives null for that coordinate. these are independent usage bands, not observed stable lines/pairs. comparative context tiles use the raw minute-valued means instead. both share one producer, source coverage and counterpart weights.

### tracking and skating admission boundary

the twenty-two tracking-dependent fields remain `missing_source`, with their particular required observation named. a public definition is not an admitted event stream. source-tagged credits follow the captured provider protocol, including pressure, possession, linked-action and sampling rules; no arbitrary geometric proxy silently fills them. primary-pass valuation is our explicitly independent reference, not the provider's tracking-enriched xg. inferred setting/territory remain separate card components.

any later tracking adapter must provide immutable source/version/rights receipt, player/game/season identity, observed windows and missing windows, actor/action credits, relevant links/classifications and denominator coverage. absence of a pass row cannot establish an unassisted play. expected-assist rates require assisted/unassisted/unknown dispositions and matching tracking windows; unusable links or recipient neutral probabilities remove affected counts and tracked time together. retain a legitimate observed primary passer who has since left the ice; do not replace tracking credit rules with event-time membership. imported aggregates require numerator/denominator and protocol identity, not merely percentiles; raw provider values cannot be relabeled our model. source-valued puck management additionally needs its model/reference/eligible-play identity. no generic importer, paid acquisition or invented private formula is assigned by this spec; the unavailable-field contract is ready, an actual source adapter needs its acquired schema.

[v1-04](v1-04-skating-source-feasibility.md) fixes skating's bounded feasibility and acquisition handoff: at most twelve production-client probes, with three initial historical detail requests and conditional source inspection. verify available primary fields, units, regular-season identity and enumeration before proposing broader capture; the user chose no automatic collection. preserve originals through the existing capture/receipt owner. a failed access probe stays an access limitation, not zero and not proof of permanent impossibility. label all situations unless the acquired source establishes a narrower scope. do not divide bursts by reconstructed 5v5 time. raw measures may display before a cohort exists; numeric skating percentiles require a checked census of source-listed regular-season players of the same F/D group with a nonnull measurement for that year, otherwise remain unavailable. no reconstructed 5v5 cutoff applies to this all-situations source cohort; publish its included/excluded player counts and acquisition coverage. exact reference composite parity is not claimed.

v1-04 owns the finite machine-readable sample/census declaration and its attributed source check; a successful sample does not complete collection. enumeration and per-measurement acquisition are separate, with unknown positions and source-only players preserved. later acquisition requires its own assignment; v1-17 integrates the resulting observations/census with publication/cohorts. schema7 source provenance alone does not certify completeness. [the recorded gap](../issues/microstat-inputs.md) names the required evidence; [v1-14](v1-14-history-cohorts-exports.md) retains raw values and `census_unverified` standings until the source population is checked and transported. no new schema or source acquisition is part of the shared history slice.
