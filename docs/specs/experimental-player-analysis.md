## minimum analytical contracts

before the corresponding implementation, define these together with its content designer; do not create a separate approval process for every coefficient:

1. **target and population:** event/stint/person-season meaning, strength, time support, units, sign, training cutoff and permitted reconstruction evidence. include zero-event exposure; numerator and denominator must match.
2. **outcome construction:** one conditional probability tree, normalized origin mass and treatment of unknown outcomes. actor-conditioned event predictions differ from reference-standardized opportunity. integrate a product over its declared joint reference; do not multiply independently averaged stages.
3. **reference and history:** league, peer, execution and replacement references have different purposes. specify centering, annual borrowing, entry/age assumptions and retrospective versus predictive use. existing fitted artifacts are reusable only under compatible contracts.
4. **attribution response:** value events before aggregating into matched stint responses, retaining shot type and context. multiplying an old attempt-effect map by average cell xg supplies only that narrower location-weighted quantity, not the full chance-valued player effect.
5. **credit ledger:** which components add, multiply, explain another term or remain separate. assign interactions/residuals explicitly. shootiness, conditional outcome stages, grouped summaries and transition subskills are not extra contributions to sum again.
6. **closure:** maps integrate to their scalar, season/game observations reconcile, and rates convert to accumulated/reference-workload values under the stated denominator. preserve definition and limitations in every display/export.

reuse 03p's probability mechanisms, 04n's rate/history mechanisms, retained features and existing source/publication owners where their contracts fit. neither prior `not_admitted` status nor a negative marginal experiment prohibits an explicitly selected experimental baseline. neither permits relabeling recorded rates as xg or a rejected scientific claim as supported. reuse is a deliberate recipe choice, not automatic promotion of old artifacts.

simple initial estimators are acceptable; meaningless proxies are not. implement compatible groups jointly and assess the assembled model. maintain named recipes and retained extraction for refitted ablations. avoid indiscriminate cross-products and duplicate encodings. broad feature use introduces collinearity, missingness and computation costs; simpler baselines and visible consequence checks help direct refinement.

## acceptance and iteration

**software acceptance:** real source-to-screen paths satisfy their construction, units, population, chronology and accounting; no invalid probability arithmetic, hidden clipping, incompatible artifact reuse or invented evidence. distinguish signed effects from impossible complete negative rates. a failed physical-rate construction may be exposed diagnostically, not silently presented as expected chances. this restriction applies to that quantity, not valid signed effects or unrelated components. numerically approximate estimates identify their method and any unresolved numerical limitation; failed solves are not successful estimates.

**experimental completion:** every selected core family has actual numerical outputs on its declared input population and the required views. partial deliveries may expose coverage gaps and blocked families; an unimplemented family keeps the program incomplete unless the user explicitly changes scope. recorded evidence, model values, metadata and exports reconcile; detached browsing and ordinary explicit publication/rollback work. integration begins before completion; a placeholder wall does not satisfy this milestone.

**scientific quality:** calibration, prediction, recovery, sensitivity, uncertainty and known failures accompany the baseline. weak results do not automatically disable an honestly labeled experimental output. claims of calibrated intervals, meaningful individual separation, portability or supported ability require appropriate additional evidence. historical assessments remain exposed and their verdicts unchanged.

use temporary end-to-end/live red → green → refactor → green checks for assigned behavior, then delete campaign-only tests/controllers and unused test dependencies. retain fixtures and checked facts; only explicitly selected stable regressions under [07](07-lightweight-testing.md) become lasting tests. adversarial review checks source semantics, numerical accounting and content, proportionate to the changed boundary.
