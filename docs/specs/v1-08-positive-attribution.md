## three objectives and annual priors

use the [positive volume/shape/quality equations and annual offsets](experimental-attribution.md#positive-volume-and-forty-mode-shape). `v=exp(eta_v)` is attempts/hour, `p=softmax(log(pi)+B theta)` distributes them, `Q=sigmoid(logit(qbar)+eta_q)` estimates neutral chance conditional on location/context. `lambda_A=v*p`; `lambda_X=lambda_A*Q`. events supply `o` and `o*q0`. quality is fractional-probability regression, not newly observed goals. mathematically `0<Q<1`; the finite representation may round its upper bound to one, so validation permits `lambda_X<=lambda_A`, but zero/nonfinite complete cell rates fail. never clip to manufacture positivity.

```text
L_volume = sum_j E[j]*exp(offset[j]+X_time[j] beta)
           - sum_e (offset[e]+X_event[e] beta) + prior
L_shape = -sum_e,c o[e,c]*log(p[e,c]) + prior
L_quality = -sum_e,c o[e,c]*(q0[e,c]*log(Q[e,c])
                           +(1-q0[e,c])*log(1-Q[e,c])) + prior
```
