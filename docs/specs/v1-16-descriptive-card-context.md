## outcome and boundary

replace the older five-rate card with the complete descriptive skater sheet inside the existing `view=observed` profile and comparison. preserve comparative cards/value discovery as the default. display actual published facts, separate populations and complete context; retain existing recorded distributions, maps, game evidence and shared-minute tables around the sheet.

schema7 already supplies `SkaterDescriptive`, `SkaterContext`, annual identities and history. use those finalized quantities; no new wire schema, endpoint, database, numerical reducer, dependency or chart framework. software acceptance can finish detached on coherent fixtures; real card completion needs [v1-03](v1-03-factual-card-reductions.md)'s verified candidate. neither depends on chance/attribution research. preserve source evidence, fits, live build, databases and listener.

accepted tradeoffs: the dense sheet needs several labeled denominators; three empirical peer guides and one factual discovery preset add a selected-season descriptive read; supporting evidence lives outside the compact png. our card describes one season, with separate three-season history, unadjusted results and dressed-game off-ice exposure. these deliberately differ from the historical [hockeyviz guide](https://hockeyviz.com/howto/skaterCard)'s multiseason, adjusted, broader off-ice construction. use [r01](../research/visual-references/README.md#inspected-examples) as composition evidence, not a numerical recipe or bundled asset.

non-goals: configurable tables, numeric descriptive percentiles, fitted skill/causal labels, new maps, interactive together/apart or reciprocal views, public hosting, combined-pair pngs, comparative-export repair and activation. the broader context destination remains unfinished; this slice closes the descriptive crosswalk, not every context view.

### presentation and scales

the designer prepares the content/scale table and one complete, partial and long-name composition before implementation. use the existing paper/teal/slate system. hierarchy: identity; usage/scoring bars; the two on/off scatters; lower conversion/penalty/deployment bars beside the counterpart scatter; compact interpretation/footer. a table of numbers alone fails acceptance.

- usage has two scales: reported minutes/appearance with its guide, and absolute included ev/pp/pk/other minutes. the stack may sum those four disjoint published seconds for display, excluding the overlapping 5v5 child/exact states; all four known → total, otherwise label a known subtotal with missing categories. never add reported time. scoring uses separate ev and pp per60 scales; corresponding panels across compared players share extents. goals/a1 sum to the published primary-point value; do not recompute the hockey total in the browser.
- each on/off panel has equal x/y rate scales, includes zero and all available points, and reverses only the screen y-axis. show a for=against reference line and distinct labeled on/off/league glyphs; no good/bad quadrant shading. attempt and goal panels have independent units/extents. corresponding panels in comparison share extents. `share_minus_half.value` is already percentage points: never multiply it by 100 again. fractions elsewhere are converted to percent exactly once.
- conversion shows positive fractions with consistent scales. penalties retain positive recorded rates; no favorable-sign inversion. starts show all five portions of 100%; score's three known portions form 100%, with unknown coverage outside that bar. never turn unavailable totals into a full bar.
- counterpart x is mean defense band, d3→d1 left to right; y is mean forward band, f1→f4 top to bottom. axes say usage band. retain fractional coordinates and distinct teammate/opponent markers. missing forward/defense evidence leaves the point unavailable, with known evidence beside it; no invented league point. band populations do not change with viewing cutoffs.
- print units and short support keys beside panels. detail resolves every key to population, denominator, missingness, method/source and reference. do not force one included-minute total across the card. group keys only when their actual meanings and support agree.

team/league reference values come directly from the producer. attempt/goal references count physical team-time; deployment references use their declared player-time/start populations. trades use the producer's dressed-team references. no browser team averages, exposure reconstruction, correction for score or recalculation from rounded figures.
