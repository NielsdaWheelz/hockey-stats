### meaning and arithmetic

- common-reference stopping and actual-opportunity saving answer different questions. neither fills the other's missing field. stopping excludes focal miss effects; do not sum subskills or reconstruct it from focal prefix goal mass.
- raw source counts may exceed paired eligibility. source `unblocked=misses+saves+goals`, `on_net=saves+goals`; paired `saved=expected−goals` uses its own matched population. expected save fraction exists only on-net. reported boxscore labels are not proof of exact physical strength.
- conditional levels are fractions; display as percentages once. differences are fractions rendered as percentage points once. peer standing always increases with supplied quantity; conversion and follow-up are not automatically favorable when high. population distributions are not confidence intervals.
- recorded stop is a source proxy, not ordinary save percentage. recorded reattempt requires the **immediately next recorded event** to be an ordinary same-team attempt in the same period, within 0–5 seconds; never skip an intervening event. it is not demonstrated physical rebound control. conditional successor danger and joint danger remain distinct; joint expectation is `E[h*d]`, not a product of separately averaged quantities.
- `ev` pools 5v5/4v4/3v3; `pk` pools defending 4v5/3v5/3v4. modeled ratios use pooled common-panel masses; observed groups use actual records. `covered_all` is supported actual-state coverage, not complete coverage or ev+pk. it has no modeled coefficient or cohort.
- qualification minutes are current supported goalie elapsed time, not precision or likelihood-event counts. entry, borrowed, mixed-current and fully-current support remain distinguishable. use existing aggregate support; never sum per-head counts to invent an event union.
